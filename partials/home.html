Rules for Creating Effective Typography

Typography shapes how users read and trust your content. Pair readable body fonts with distinctive headings, respect hierarchy, and keep rhythm with consistent spacing. Line-height between 1.4–1.6 improves legibility; use scale that adapts across breakpoints, and maintain contrast that meets accessibility.
(Design Shack, 2025)

Figma Auto Layout

Auto Layout unlocks reusable components that flex to content and screens—similar to CSS Flexbox. Combine padding, gap, and alignment with “hug”/“fill” to scale quickly while keeping developer handoff clean. Variants + Auto Layout form the backbone of robust design systems.
(Design Shack, 2025)

Understanding Stock Image Licenses

Licenses determine where and how you can use imagery—from editorial-only to extended commercial. Read restrictions, watch for trademarks and faces, and keep records of attribution when required. When in doubt, choose reputable libraries with clear terms.
(Design Shack, 2025)
